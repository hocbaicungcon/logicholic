About Logicholic

Logicholic offers free logic games and puzzles, including Sudoku, 2048, Minesweeper, Nonogram, Kakuro, and more.

What you can play

Logicholic brings together more than 20 puzzle and logic games. The collection includes Sudoku, 2048, Minesweeper, Gomoku, Nim, mazes, Nonogram, Slitherlink, Bridges, Klotski, Light Up, Kakuro, Futoshiki, and more.

Many games offer different difficulty levels, curated challenges, or generated puzzles. Hints explain the reasoning behind a move so you can learn as you play.

How the site works

The games are free to play in a browser without creating an account or installing an app. They work with a mouse, touch controls, or a keyboard where supported.

Choose from dark, light, warm paper, and E-Ink themes. The layout adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens. The website is available in English.

Who it is for

New players can start with easier levels, while experienced players can explore harder puzzles and advanced Sudoku practice. The site is also a place to enjoy a short mental break.

For questions, feedback, or reports of a puzzle issue, email admin@logicholic.com.
